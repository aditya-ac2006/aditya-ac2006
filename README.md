## Hey, I'm Aditya 👋

I'm a **Computer Science Engineering student at Manipal Institute of Technology Bengaluru (MAHE)**, interested in the point where software meets complex, real-world systems.

My work currently spans **vehicle dynamics and tire modelling**, **data-driven software**, and **finance/fintech**. I enjoy taking noisy or complicated problems, building a structured pipeline around them, and turning the result into something that can actually support decisions.

## Featured work

### [CMM — Tire Modelling & Vehicle Dynamics](https://github.com/aditya-ac2006/CMM)

A MATLAB-based Formula Student engineering pipeline for converting tire-test data into validated lateral tire models and, eventually, a complete vehicle-dynamics and lap-time simulation environment.

**What the project currently demonstrates**

- Reproducible data validation, loading and characterization stages
- Sweep-level quality control and operating-point analysis
- Lateral tire-response characterization and Magic Formula modelling
- Separation of raw data, engineering contracts, model fitting and validation
- An architecture designed to grow into vehicle modelling and simulation

`MATLAB` `Vehicle Dynamics` `Data Validation` `Tire Modelling` `Formula Student`
